# Theory of Cauchy Equations

Theory of Cauchy Equations is a focused analytical monograph exploring one of the most elegant and influential structures in functional analysis and modern mathematics: the Cauchy functional equation.

Beginning with the classical additive equation, the text gradually develops the theory through generalized forms, differentiable and analytic solutions, transformable equations, multidimensional extensions, and structural interpretations.

Written in a clear and systematic style, this monograph emphasizes intuition, structure, and mathematical understanding while maintaining analytical rigor throughout.

Part of the Advanced Monograph Series, this book is designed as a compact yet substantial exposition of a subject whose deceptively simple equations conceal remarkable mathematical depth.
